Our Story

Rooted in Passion,
Grown with Care

From a small balcony garden to a full botanical studio — this is the story of Leaf & Bloom.

The beginning

Started in Cebu

In 2018, founder Johnnel Artiaga began delivering potted plants to neighbours in Cebu City. That small route became Leaf & Bloom — a studio for plants, florals, and event work.

Today the studio serves homes, workplaces, and celebrations across the Philippines, with pricing in pesos and care notes that match the climate.

We choose plants that will live in the room they enter — not only look good for a photograph.

Johnnel

Founder & Lead Botanist

What we do

Mission

Bring well-chosen plants and florals into Philippine homes, workplaces, and celebrations — with clear care and honest pricing in pesos.

Studio values

Open a value to see how it shows up in the weekly work.

Plants are checked before packing. Arrangements are finished in the studio, not assembled in a rush at the door.

We work with growers we trust and keep packaging simple and reusable where we can.

If a plant will not thrive in your light, we say so — and suggest another.

Briefs start with the room, the date, and the budget before any design language.

Stems and foliage are placed for scale and use — not for filling empty space.

New cultivars, seasons, and care methods stay part of the weekly studio work.
