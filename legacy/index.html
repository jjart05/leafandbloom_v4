About the studio

Grown from a balcony garden in Cebu

Leaf & Bloom began in 2018 when Johnnel Artiaga started sharing carefully potted plants with neighbours in Buhisan. The studio now works across home collections, wedding florals, and workplace planting.

We select for Philippine light and living spaces — not catalog glitter. Every arrangement starts with a conversation about the room, the event, and the care it will need.
